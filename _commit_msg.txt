Verify the full TTS path through the sidecar; fix byte/char wording

The one seam nothing had exercised was a real render driven through the
sidecar rather than direct. AI/integration_generate.py closes it: it started
a 52-chunk job as a child process, watched every chunk progress from 0 to
100% through the server's log parser, and confirmed the parsed @@RESULT
duration (1417.29 s) against ffprobe (1417.289708 s). 16.6 minutes wall
clock.

Two things surfaced while building it:

- /generate hardcoded output.wav/output.mp3, so rendering a variant would
  have overwritten the approved session. It now accepts out_wav/out_mp3, and
  the integration test writes to a scratch path and asserts the real
  output.mp3 survived.
- piece_table.h called its offsets "chars". std::string addresses bytes, and
  a false claim in a header is how someone writes a UTF-8 bug later. The
  header now says bytes; engine and ABI suites rebuilt and still pass.

README and architecture.md updated to distinguish verified from unverified,
and the integration test is documented as the slow companion to verify.py.
